# Product case studies

Written accounts of products I have owned as Technical Product Manager or Technical Project Manager. Each one covers what the problem actually was, the constraints I was working inside, what I owned, the part that went wrong, and where it landed.

They are deliberately not success stories. The useful part of a case study is the decision that was hard and the thing that broke.

## A note on what is missing

Most of my delivery history sits under client NDA, roughly 35 products across PropTech, LegalTech, GovTech, InsurTech, e-commerce, travel and marketplace verticals. Those are not written up here because I cannot name them, and an anonymised case study with the specifics removed is not worth reading.

The four above are products I am free to discuss by name. They are representative rather than exhaustive.
