# Product case studies

Written accounts of products I have owned as Technical Product Manager, Product Manager or Technical Project Manager. Each one covers what the problem actually was, the constraints I was working inside, what I owned, the part that went wrong or the decision that was hard, and where it landed.

They are deliberately not success stories. The useful part of a case study is the decision that was difficult and the thing that broke.

## What each one is actually about

The sector labels are the least interesting thing on this page. What these are really about:

- **EdgeCards** and **Mediwrite**: money moving under regulation, and what the product does when it does not know whether it moved
- **SoccerNet 3.0**: where a platform ends and tenant configuration begins, and what happens when you draw that line in the wrong place
- **ResearchCollab** and **AfCEN**: where AI earns its place in a product and where it must be kept out, when the user cannot check the answer
- **AphiaOne**: why the documented workflow and the real workflow are different, and which one is the requirement
- **Breach monitoring**: alert fatigue as a first-class failure mode, not an afterthought
- **iMe Work**: building for the user when someone else is the buyer
