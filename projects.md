## How to read this list

The spread is the point. Six years across nine sectors means the pattern recognition transfers: a settlement corridor and an insurance claims flow have the same failure shape, and a multi-tenant sports platform and a marketplace have the same boundary problem.

The depth is in the case studies. A table of sixty-one names proves volume, not judgement. The eight linked write-ups are where the actual decisions are.
